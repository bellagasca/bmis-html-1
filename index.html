Kona Gasca

Meet Kona, my baby dog who is the cutest ever.

Kona is an Australian Cattle-Sybirian Husky mix, she mandates that anyone she meets must rub her belly and give all of their attention to her. She is a moody pup who does not enjoy long walks because she wants to sleep in the most inconvient spots around the house.

Kona is about 3 and a half years old. She was the runt of her liter and was the only one not picked because she had brown eyes, unlike all of her siblings who had blue. She has more attitude every day because she knows that she is the princess of the house.

Three facts about Kona

  • Kona only hates three things: smaller dogs, fireworks, and our gardener George.
  • She answers to other names such as: Patones, Chunky, Mamas, and Konies
  • Her favorite movie is 101 Dalmations.
